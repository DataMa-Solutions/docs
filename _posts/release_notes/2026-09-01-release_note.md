* **Marketplace extension** (also known as "Datama Light") — **Datama Compare redesign**
  * **Algorithm**: choose how the waterfall explores your data — **Steps then Dimensions**, **Dimensions then Steps or Dimensions**, or **Auto** (picks the most insightful path). **Infinite drill-down** lets you keep splitting by dimension as deep as your data allows; drill in / drill up stay fluid with morphing bars and synced smart title, subtitle, and comment.
  * **Dimension hierarchy**: define natural orders (e.g. Country → Region → City), open on the right level by default, drill or filter deeper, and **pin** a dimension as the priority driver.
  * **Display**: pin an interactive **Table** next to the waterfall (copy as CSV); add synthetic **pillars** on the chart (right-click → **Add pillar**) to stage checkpoints in your story; place **difference arrows** between bars (think-cell style, anchored to data).
  * **Workspace & settings**: pin **Waterfall**, **Comment**, **Tree**, and **Table** side by side — hover, drill, and Split by stay in sync across views. Settings live in a searchable side panel (collapsed by default); edit labels, connectors, and display options from the **right-click context menu** or shortcut bar on hover.
  * **Tree extras**: show market-equation **operators** and step **emojis** on cards. Keyboard shortcuts: **Shift + R** (reverse axes), **Shift + W** / **Shift + T** (Waterfall ↔ Tree).
