What would happen to your KPI if conversion improved by 10%? 🔮

With **What If** in the Datama Compare extension, simulate it on your own data and read the answer in the usual **Waterfall**, **Tree** or **Table**. No spreadsheet on the side.

How to do it 👇

* ⚙️ Open **Settings › What If** and add a scenario: give it a name and pick the **baseline** (whole dataset, or a dimension with selected segments)
* 🎯 Add a **KPI impact**: choose a step of your market equation, an optional segment scope, and a **relative (%)** or **absolute** change
* ✅ Turn on **Use as comparison**, then **Apply**: the waterfall now compares baseline vs scenario
* 🖱️ Faster: right-click a waterfall bar to create a scenario already prefilled with the step and segment
* 📌 **Pin** an impact to get a live shortcut on the visual

✨ Know which lever is worth pulling before you pull it.
