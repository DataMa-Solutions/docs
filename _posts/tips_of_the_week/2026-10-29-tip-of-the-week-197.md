Want the audience to see the gap, not just read it? 🏹

In Datama Compare, add a think-cell style **Difference arrow** between two bars of your **Waterfall**. It stays anchored to the data, so it survives drill, reverse axis and layout changes.

How to do it 👇

* 🖱️ **Right-click** the **Waterfall** and choose **Annotate › Difference arrow**
* 📏 Link two bars: the **level difference** between neighbours, or the **total difference** across a range of steps
* 📈 Optional: a **CAGR-style** growth arrow
* 🎨 **Click the line** to cycle through the three style presets
* 🔢 **Click the label** to switch between **% change**, **impact in points** and **both**

✨ The key gap of your story, highlighted in one slide-ready visual.
