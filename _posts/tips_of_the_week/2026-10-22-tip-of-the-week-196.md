Still reaching for the mouse every time you change view? ⌨️

The redesigned Datama Compare extension has four keyboard shortcuts. Flip your story from **Waterfall** to **Tree**, reverse the axes or jump straight to a setting, without leaving the keyboard.

Try them 👇

* 🔄 **Shift + R**: reverse the axes of the **Waterfall** and / or the **Tree**
* 📊 **Shift + W**: switch to the **Waterfall** view
* 🌳 **Shift + T**: switch from the **Waterfall** to the **Tree**
* 🔎 **Shift + S**: open the **settings panel** with the cursor already in the search bar, which also finds the options of the analysis panels (Comparison, Modeling, Dimensions...)

✨ Faster live demos, and the right setting one search away.
