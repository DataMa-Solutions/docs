Too many bars on your waterfall? 📊

In Datama Compare, only the **most impacting bars** stay visible. The rest are grouped into a single **Remaining** bar, so your story stays readable and nothing is lost.

How to do it 👇

* 🔢 **Settings › Compare › Items displayed**: choose how many bars stay visible before **Remaining** (6 by default, up to 50)
* 🖱️ **Click Remaining** to expand more elements
* ↩️ **Right-click › Regroup remaining** to collapse them again
* 🧮 **Group equation steps**: turn it on to let market equation steps be folded into **Remaining** too (off by default)

✨ A clean slide for your audience, and the full detail one click away.
