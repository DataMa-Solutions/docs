Want your waterfall to tell a clearer story to stakeholders? 🧑‍💻

With '**Sub-pillars**' on the Datama Compare extension, add **checkpoints** 🚩 on the waterfall, without changing your market equation.

How to use them:

* Right-click a bar → '**Add pillar**' (before or after)
* Name the checkpoint (e.g. “After mix”, “After price”) and choose the impact reference (vs previous net or vs Start)
* Edit them later under **Waterfall › Elements › Pillars**, or remove them with a right-click then select '**Remove pillar**'

✨ Perfect to stage the narrative of the variation step by step.
