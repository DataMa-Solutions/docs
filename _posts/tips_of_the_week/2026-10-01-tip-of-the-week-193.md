A step of your market equation is itself a mini-funnel? 🧩

With '**Sub-steps**' on the Datama Compare extension, nest an equation inside a step (e.g. Conversion = Add to Cart × Cart to Order) instead of flattening it by hand.

How to use them:

* Open **Settings › Market Equation** and build your steps with the **card editor**: hover a card → '**Add a sub-step**', indent / outdent, edit the formula, add an emoji
* On the waterfall, right-click a composite bar → '**Split by › Sub-steps**' to open it into its child sub-steps
* Steps that are not a simple ratio can only be split by Sub-steps, so the decomposition always stays valid

✨ Perfect to explain a variation down to the smallest stage of your funnel. Not to be confused with Sub-pillars, which only add storytelling checkpoints without changing the KPI math.
