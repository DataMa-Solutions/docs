Is "Other" the biggest bar on your chart? 🧺

Datama pools small segments into "Other" to keep your visuals readable. On a dimension with many small values (cities, stores, SKUs), "Other" can end up weighing more than your top segment. **Long tail** fixes that: it pools only the smallest segments, as long as their total stays under your threshold.

How to do it 👇

* ⚙️ Open **Settings**, then the **Clustering** section
* 🎯 Set the **Threshold**: at 5%, "Other" never weighs more than 5% of your KPI
* 🧮 Switch **Method** from **Smallest segments** to **Long tail**, then click **Apply**
* 🧩 Available in **Explore**, **Compare**, **Detect** and **Assess**

✨ Your real drivers stay visible, and "Other" goes back to being a footnote.
