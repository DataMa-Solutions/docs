Three new powers just landed in Datama Compare (and beyond) 🚀

* **What If scenarios** 🔮 : simulate "what happens to my KPI if I improve step X by 10% on segment Y", and read the answer straight through your usual Waterfall, Tree, or Table. Pin an impact as a live shortcut and watch the chart recompute as you drag it. Available on Compare, Explore, Assess, and Pivot.
* **Sub-steps** 🧩 : break any market-equation step into its own mini equation (e.g. Conversion Rate = Add to Cart Rate x Cart to Order Rate), then right-click and Split by › Sub-steps to see what really drove it.
* **Covariance, tamed** ⚖️ : decide per step whether it should Always, Never, or Auto absorb the covariance, isolate it in its own dedicated bar, and get a friendly warning when it gets too high to compare apples to apples.
