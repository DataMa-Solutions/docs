Three new powers just landed in Datama Compare 🚀

* **What If scenarios** 🔮 : simulate a KPI impact and watch it flow through your Waterfall, Tree, or Table.
* **Sub-steps** 🧩 : break a step into its own mini equation, then split its bar by sub-step.
* **Covariance, tamed** ⚖️ : choose per step if it should catch the covariance, or isolate it in its own bar.
